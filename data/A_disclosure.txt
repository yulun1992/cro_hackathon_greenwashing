Verdant Manufacturing — SYNTHETIC 2025 disclosure. Fictional company and figures.

Total Scope 1 and 2 emissions increased from 100,000 tCO2e in 2024 to 108,000 tCO2e in 2025. Emissions intensity per tonne of product decreased by 30% in the same period. Boundaries and methods are unchanged between years.

In 2025, renewable electricity accounted for 60% of electricity consumed by all owned factories. The remaining 40% was non-renewable electricity. The figures cover all owned factories and the full year.

Our target is net zero emissions for owned operations by 2040. Net zero has not yet been achieved.
